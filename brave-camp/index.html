Brave Camp 2025
2025
Visual identity and social campaign for Brave Camp 2025 by Today, I'm Brave, developed at David&Goliath for Today, I'm Brave. The project introduces a bold, modular brand system that extends across digital touchpoionts, translating the organization's mission into a bold and recognizable visual language.
Design
The identity centers on a high-contrast palette with bold typographic hierarchy. Iconography and graphic motifs were developed to represent the camp’s core values, creating a recognizable and repeatable language across posts, stories, and campaign materials.
Outcome
  • A cohesive social media campaign across Instagram posts and stories
  • A flexible visual identity system adaptable across multiple content types
  • Branded assets designed to increase engagement and support signups and donations
