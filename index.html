KATELYN LEE

Katelyn Lee is a LA-based designer who loves to ask questions and make work that invites people to take a closer look. She works across branding, editorial, motion, and digital design, built on research, storytelling, and what she knows is an eyebrow-raising number of open tabs (now at 212).

She's designed for global agencies, boutique studios, nonprofits, and independent artists in Los Angeles, New York, and Seoul.
